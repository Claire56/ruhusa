# Ruhusa v0.6-C — Fail-Closed Recovery for Uncertain Execution

**Document status:** Validated research note  
**Milestone:** v0.6-C  
**Release target:** v0.6.0  
**Verification status:** VALIDATED — 118 tests passed; v0.6.0 build succeeded

---

## 1. Research Question

> How should an autonomous AI-agent workflow recover when an execution claim
> becomes stale or the outcome of an external side effect is uncertain?

v0.6-C starts from two earlier findings:

```text
operation binding != execution uniqueness
authorization-time validity != execution-time validity
```

It adds a third:

> **Execution-attempt uniqueness does not imply side-effect uniqueness.**

---

## 2. Failure Model

```text
authorize
   |
claim
   |
revalidate
   |
external side effect
   |
worker crash / response loss / timeout
   |
CLAIMED or UNKNOWN
```

Loss of the worker does not prove that the external side effect failed.

Therefore Ruhusa must not silently turn abandoned execution authority back into
retryable authority.

---

## 3. v0.6-C Controls

### Stale-claim quarantine

`mark_stale_claim_unknown()` moves a sufficiently stale `CLAIMED` execution to:

```text
UNKNOWN
```

never directly to `AVAILABLE`.

This preserves fail-closed behavior when the external outcome cannot be known.

### Explicit reconciliation

`reconcile_unknown()` accepts two recovery outcomes:

```text
SIDE_EFFECT_CONFIRMED
    UNKNOWN -> COMPLETED

SIDE_EFFECT_NOT_APPLIED
    UNKNOWN -> AVAILABLE
```

A return to `AVAILABLE` does not itself execute the action. A later attempt
must obtain a fresh execution claim.

### Process-local recovery atomicity

The in-memory store applies claim and recovery transitions under the same lock,
so concurrent reconciliation produces at most one successful transition in the
tested process.

## 5. Critical Trust Assumption

The merged v0.6-C implementation does **not** cryptographically authenticate or
independently verify the provenance of the reconciliation outcome.

The method itself states that it belongs to trusted reconciliation
infrastructure.

Therefore:

```text
agent self-asserts SIDE_EFFECT_NOT_APPLIED
        !=
trusted evidence that the side effect did not occur
```

An application must not expose `reconcile_unknown()` as an unrestricted
agent-callable capability.

This is an explicit v0.6 boundary and a future research/production target.

---

## 6. What v0.6-C Establishes

Under the tested process-local model:

- stale claims fail closed into `UNKNOWN`;
- live claims are not stolen before the configured stale threshold;
- `UNKNOWN` does not automatically become retryable;
- confirmed completion permanently consumes the invocation;
- confirmed non-execution can permit a fresh execution claim;
- reconciliation is valid only from `UNKNOWN`;
- concurrent recovery has one winner;
- stale permits cannot mutate a recovered/fresh execution attempt;
- malformed recovery parameters are rejected.

---

## 7. Explicit Non-Claims

v0.6-C does not establish:

- authenticated provenance of recovery evidence;
- correctness of the external recovery source;
- distributed recovery atomicity across processes or pods;
- durable execution state;
- exactly-once external side effects;
- downstream idempotency;
- transactional authorization plus remote side effect;
- automatic reconciliation of `UNKNOWN`;
- safe blind retry when the external outcome is unknowable.

---

## 8. Dissertation Relevance

Autonomous agents retry tools, pause, crash, replan, and recover asynchronously.
A system that simply retries after loss of execution state can amplify a single
human-authorized action into multiple real-world side effects.

The v0.6 research progression is therefore:

```text
operation binding
    != execution uniqueness

authorization-time validity
    != execution-time validity

execution-time revalidation
    != atomic authorization + external side effect

execution-attempt uniqueness
    != side-effect uniqueness

self-asserted recovery outcome
    != trusted execution evidence
```

The final distinction is intentionally documented as a remaining trust
boundary rather than a solved guarantee.
