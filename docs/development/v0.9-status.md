## Approval security boundary

`REQUIRE_APPROVAL` denotes an authorization outcome. It is not proof that a
specific human approved a particular invocation. Any durable approval
implementation must bind the exact invocation/operation, enforce expiry and
single-use consumption, and receive authenticated approver identity from
trusted infrastructure. A stored identity string alone is not authentication.
